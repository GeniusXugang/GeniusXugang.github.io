#### Biography

I am Xugang Zheng (郑旭刚), a Ph.D. candidate in Labor Economics at the School of Economics, Dongbei University of Finance and Economics. My research focuses on labor economics, urban economics, agricultural economics, and regional development, with particular attention to new-type urbanization, migrant workers, county-level development, youth employment, and the citizenization of agricultural transfer populations.

我目前为东北财经大学经济学院劳动经济学专业博士研究生。主要研究方向包括劳动经济学、城市经济学、农业经济学与区域经济学，重点关注新型城镇化、农业转移人口市民化、县域发展、青年就业以及劳动力市场政策效应。
